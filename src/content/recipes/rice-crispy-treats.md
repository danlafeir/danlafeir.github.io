---
title: "Rice Crispy Treats"
description: "Chewy squares made with browned butter, a little vanilla, and a pinch of salt."
---

Makes 12 squares.

## Ingredients

- 2 sticks (226 grams) unsalted butter
- 2 (10-ounce) bags mini marshmallows
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- ½ teaspoon kosher salt
- 9 cups (225 grams) crispy rice cereal

## Instructions

1. Grease a 9×13-inch baking pan with nonstick spray or a little butter.
2. Melt the butter in a large stainless steel pot over medium-low heat, swirling occasionally. It will foam and crackle. When the crackling stops, keep swirling until it smells nutty and amber bits form on the bottom, 2 to 3 minutes.
3. Drop the heat to low. Add the marshmallows, vanilla, and salt, and stir constantly until the marshmallows have completely melted. Take the pot off the heat.
4. Stir in the cereal until it is evenly coated.
5. Scrape the mixture into the pan and press it into an even layer with a spatula or damp fingers. Press gently, just enough to hold it together. The harder you pack it, the denser and less chewy it gets.
6. Let cool to room temperature, about 1 hour, then cut into 12 squares.

Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 days.
